## 1. Overview & Chamber Breakdown

The Temple of the Eternal Sun is a 7-chamber handcrafted ancient complex:

1. **Portal of Astral Light (`chamber_temple_entrance`)**: Entrance archway with sun carving murals.
2. **Collapsed Sun Court (`chamber_sun_court`)**: Sunken court with rune mosaic puzzles.
3. **Sacred Botanical Sanctuary (`chamber_sacred_gardens`)**: Luminescent flora and herbalist tablets.
4. **Observatory of the Ancients (`chamber_observatory`)**: Celestial brass orrery mapping the stars.
5. **Water & Prism Hall (`chamber_water_prism`)**: Flooded marble hall with light reflection prisms.
6. **Subterranean Archive Sanctum (`chamber_subterranean_sanctum`)**: Golden codex plates.
7. **Core Astral Vault (`chamber_astral_vault`)**: Altar sanctum holding the ancient Astral Tear.
