## Act I Conclusion — World Impact
Upon completing `q_act1_conclusion`:
- **Citadel of Void Shadows** permanently sealed.
- Travel route to **Eastern Ridgeline** (Act II region gateway) unlocked.
- **Tier 2 merchants** appear in Oakvale and Elderwood Grove.
- **Sylvan Guardians** formally allied with Valen Crown.
- Shadow Cult forces retreat to Mirkwood Swamps.
- Player receives the title **"Hero of Oakvale"** and access to Tier 2 equipment.
