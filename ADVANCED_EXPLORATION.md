# HERO OF ETERNIA — ADVANCED EXPLORATION & HAZARDS GUIDE

---

## 1. Overview & Environmental Hazards

Act III exploration challenges players with complex environmental hazards requiring specific traversal tools and elemental resistance:

- **Toxic Spore Fog**: Deals damage over time unless player equips Herbal Filter Mask or uses Fire alchemy to ignite spore pockets.
- **Bottomless Chasm**: Instant fall-kill hazard requiring precise Grapple Hook timing.
- **Void Lightning Storms**: Periodic lightning strikes on open plains requiring cover behind ancient obelisks.
