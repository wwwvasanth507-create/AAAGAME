# ACT II CRAFTING EXPANSION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 31)

## Overview

After liberating the Eastern Ridgeline Watchtower, two advanced crafting stations unlock. These introduce **Tier 2** recipes and preview the first **Tier 3** recipe.

---

## Crafting Stations

| Station ID | Name | Location | Unlock Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| `station_ridgeline_forge` | Ridgeline War Forge | Ridgeline Watchtower | 20 |
| `station_mirkwood_alchemy` | Swamp Alchemy Cauldron | Mirkwood Swamps | 22 |

---

## Tier 2 Recipes

| Recipe | Output | Ingredients | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| `recipe_stormforged_sword` | Stormforged Sword | 2× Void Crystal, 4× Storm Iron, 2× Ridgeline Flint | 20 |
| `recipe_ridgeline_warplate` | Ridgeline War Plate | 6× Storm Iron, 1× Void Crystal, 3× Harpy Feather | 21 |
| `recipe_swamp_antidote` | Swamp Antidote | 3× Bog Lotus, 2× Swamp Moss | 21 |

---

## Tier 3 Preview Recipe (Unlock Level 24)

| Recipe | Output | Ingredients | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| `recipe_voidweave_cloak` | Voidweave Cloak | 4× Void Crystal, 5× Shadow Silk, 1× Moonstone | 24 |

> The Voidweave Cloak is the first Tier 3 item — a preview of Act III crafting depth.
