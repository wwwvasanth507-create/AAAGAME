# HERO OF ETERNIA — FORTRESS ENCOUNTERS & TACTICS

---

## 1. Combat & Stealth Mechanics

Fortress encounters blend strategic stealth, alarm management, and heavy squad combat:

- **Alarm Gong Destruction**: Players can sneak up to alarm gongs and sever rope cords before scouts strike them.
- **Explosive Barrel Chain Reactions**: Shooting void fire barrels in the armory clears heavy knight squads.
- **Prisoner Support**: Rescued scouts fight alongside the player during the courtyard retreat.
