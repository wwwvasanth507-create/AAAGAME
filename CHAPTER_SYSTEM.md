# CHAPTER SYSTEM SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 26)

## System Overview
`ChapterFramework.cs` and `ChapterDefinition.cs` structure campaign chapters into Prologue, Acts, Chapters, Missions, Interludes, and Finale/Expansion hooks.

## Chapter Types
* **Prologue**: Introduction and tutorial sequence.
* **Act**: Major storyline milestone grouping multiple chapters.
* **Chapter**: Discrete campaign act component.
* **Mission**: Primary narrative objective.
* **Interlude**: Side activities and transition periods.
* **FinaleHook**: Main campaign climax anchor.
* **PostGameHook**: Endgame activities unlocked after campaign completion.
* **ExpansionHook**: DLC and expansion storyline attachment points.
