# SECOND SETTLEMENT SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 29)

## Elderwood Grove (`settlement_elderwood`)

### 1. Settlement Districts (`SecondSettlementContent.cs`)
* **Warden's Great Hall**: Administrative center managed by Warden Kaelen.
* **Sylvan Marketplace**: Trade square with Guildmaster Vance and regional traders.
* **Ironwood Forge**: Advanced smithy for steel weapons and sylvan armor.
* **The Golden Leaf Inn**: Tavern resting location and rumors hub.
* **Corin's Alchemy Workshop**: High alchemist laboratory for potent potions.
* **Temple of the Sun**: Solar shrine managed by Priestess Lyanna.
