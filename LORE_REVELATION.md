## 1. The Shattered Crown Revelation

In Chapter 10, discovering the Golden Codex plate in the Subterranean Archive Sanctum delivers the central campaign turning point:

- **Historical Truth**: Arch-Sorcerer Malakor was originally High Warden Malakor, charged with guarding the Void Core.
- **The Tragic Fall**: Malakor absorbed the Void Core to prevent it from consuming Eternia, but the core's dark essence corrupted his mind, driving his campaign of conquest.
- **The Path to Act IV**: Sealing the Void Core requires forging the Astral Crown using legendary materials scattered across Act IV's high-level realms.
