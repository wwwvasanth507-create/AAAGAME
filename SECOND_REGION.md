# SECOND REGION SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 29)

## Sylvanwood Wilds (`region_forest`)

### 1. Key Locations (`SecondRegionContent.cs`)
* **Sylvanwood Main Canopy**: Central dense woodland hub with elevated wooden walkways.
* **Ruins of Aethelgard**: Ancient Elven architectural ruins containing Titan Relic chambers.
* **Mistveil River Crossing**: River rapids with fishing spots and water wheel.
* **Venomous Cavern Entrance**: Subterranean nest housing Giant Venom Spiders.
* **Stormwatch Ridge**: High mountain cliff overlooking the entire valley.
* **Northguard Spire**: Abandoned imperial watchtower with high-tier loot chests.
