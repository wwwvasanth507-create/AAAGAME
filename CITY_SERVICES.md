# HERO OF ETERNIA — CITY SERVICES & URBAN FUNCTIONALITY

---

## 1. Scope & Services Catalog

City Services provide specialized urban gameplay and progression services across Valenhold and Eternia Prime:

- **Luxury Merchants**: Rare gemstone, exotic mount armor, and cosmetic transmog vendors.
- **Imperial Vault Storage**: Expandable personal bank storage slots (up to 200 items).
- **Royal Archives**: Lore research consoles unlocking codex entries and ancient map markers.
- **Skill Respec Trainers**: Skill point re-allocation for gold fees.
- **High Healing Sanctuary**: Full vitals restoration, curse removal, and temporary health buff blessings.
