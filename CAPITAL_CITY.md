# HERO OF ETERNIA — CAPITAL CITY: ETERNIA PRIME

---

## 1. Overview & District Breakdown

Eternia Prime is the grand capital metropolis of Eternia, spanning 8 distinct urban districts:

1. **The Royal Citadel & Throne Spire (`district_capital_royal`)**: Imperial palace, high court, and diplomatic embassies.
2. **Grand Bazaar of the Sun (`district_capital_commercial`)**: Commercial marketplace, luxury merchants, and auction houses.
3. **Imperial Foundry & War Quarter (`district_capital_foundry`)**: Steam forges, master weapon smithing, and war armorers.
4. **Grand Guild Enclave (`district_capital_guild`)**: Adventurers' Guild, Mages' Arcane Circle, and Iron Artisans.
5. **Cathedral of the Eternal Light (`district_capital_temple`)**: High healing sanctuary, divine blessings, and curse removal.
6. **Old City Residential Heights (`district_capital_residential`)**: Player housing, taverns, and local artisan shops.
7. **Royal Archives & Guard Barracks (`district_capital_archives`)**: Ancient lore research, libraries, and guard barracks.
8. **The Sunken Catacombs (`district_capital_underground`)**: Subterranean tunnels, black market trading, and secret passages.
