# WORLD LORE BIBLE — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 27)

## Creation Myth & History Timeline

### 1. Creation Myth
In the beginning, the cosmos was a turbulent void until the **Primeval Titans** sculpted the realm of **Eternia**. Anchored by five elemental leyline pillars, Eternia flourished in eternal balance until the Void Lord **Malakor** attempted to drain the world's lifeblood. The Titans bound Malakor in the Abyssal Wastes using five ancient seals before ascending into legend.

### 2. Timeline of Ages
* **Age of Creation (First Age)**: Titans forge Eternia and set leyline pillars.
* **Age of Shattering (Second Age)**: Malakor's invasion, the Void War, and the sealing of the Void Lord.
* **Age of Empire (Third Age)**: High Eternian Empire unifies the continent and builds majestic stone spires.
* **Age of Decline (Fourth Age)**: Internal strife causes the Empire to crumble into independent kingdoms.
* **Age of Awakening (Current Fifth Age)**: Ancient seals begin to fracture as monsters resurge across Valenoria.
