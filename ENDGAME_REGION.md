# HERO OF ETERNIA — ENDGAME REGION GUIDE: THE ASTRAL DIVIDE

---

## 1. Overview & Sub-Zone Breakdown

The Astral Divide is a high-level 7-zone endgame realm:

1. **The Crystal Wasteland (`zone_crystal_wasteland`)**: Danger level 40, razor crystal storms.
2. **Shattered Realm of Sol (`zone_shattered_realm`)**: Danger level 41, floating golden archipelagos over a solar rift.
3. **Floating Ruins of Caelum (`zone_caelum_ruins`)**: Danger level 42, floating spires with gravity reversal zones.
4. **Celestial Rift Canyon (`zone_celestial_canyon`)**: Danger level 42, storm drakes and cosmic lightning.
5. **Ashen Astral Battlefield (`zone_astral_battlefield`)**: Danger level 43, ancient war dreadnoughts and giant broken weapons.
6. **Forgotten Sun Spire (`zone_forgotten_sun_temple`)**: Danger level 44, sun high priest lich mini-boss.
7. **Obsidian Citadel Gate (`zone_obsidian_threshold`)**: Danger level 45, Malakor shadow guards protecting the ultimate fortress threshold.
