# CHAPTER 01 SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 28)

## Chapter 1 Quest Chain (`Chapter1QuestChain.cs`)

### 1. Awakening in Oakvale (`q_oakvale_awakening`)
* **Category**: Main
* **Recommended Level**: 1
* **Objective**: Talk to Elder Alden in the village square.
* **Reward**: 100 XP, 25 Gold.

### 2. Forging a Hero's Blade (`q_forging_the_blade`)
* **Category**: Main
* **Recommended Level**: 2
* **Objective**: Gather 3 Iron Ore for Blacksmith Thorin.
* **Reward**: 150 XP, 50 Gold, Rusty Iron Sword.

### 3. The Bandit Incursion (`q_bandit_threat`)
* **Category**: Main
* **Recommended Level**: 3
* **Objective**: Defeat 5 Bandit Scouts on trade roads.
* **Reward**: 250 XP, 100 Gold.

### 4. Confronting Baron Skarr (`q_boss_skarr_encounter`)
* **Category**: Main (Mini-boss)
* **Recommended Level**: 4
* **Objective**: Defeat Bandit Leader Skarr in the Whispering Cavern.
* **Reward**: 500 XP, 250 Gold, Oakvale Defender Title.
