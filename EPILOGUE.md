# HERO OF ETERNIA — INTERACTIVE REGIONAL EPILOGUE

---

## 1. Scope & Epilogue Mechanics

The interactive epilogue permits free travel across Eternia to experience the aftermath of victory:

- **Valenhold Metropolis**: Commander Valen promotes the player to Grand Marshal of the Militia.
- **Eternia Prime Capital**: High Inquisitor Vesper and the Royal Guard host a grand victory festival in the Grand Plaza.
- **The Shadow Frontier**: Shadow Rangers begin clearing remaining corrupted outposts, opening peaceful trade routes.
