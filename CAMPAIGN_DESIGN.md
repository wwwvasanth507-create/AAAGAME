# CAMPAIGN DESIGN BLUEPRINT — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 27)

## Campaign Outline

### Prologue: Awakening in Oakvale (Levels 1–5, ~2.5 Hours)
* **Purpose**: Introduce movement, combat, inventory, dialogue, and quest systems.
* **Goals**: Defend Oakvale from a surprise bandit/shadow raid; discover the dormant Eternia mark.
* **Featured Region**: Valenoria (`region_starting_kingdom`).

### Act I: Shadows over Valenoria (Levels 5–18, ~12 Hours)
* **Purpose**: Establish regional factions, corrupted shrines, and the main antagonist threat.
* **Goals**: Purify Sylvanwood Wilds shrines; defeat Baron Skarr; uncover Malakor's Void Gate plot.
* **Featured Regions**: Valenoria, Sylvanwood Wilds, Mirkwood Swamps.

### Act II: Flame and Frost (Levels 18–32, ~20 Hours)
* **Purpose**: Expand world exploration, elemental interactions, and faction diplomacy.
* **Goals**: Retrieve Titan Relics from Sunfire Wastes and Frostpeak Mountains.
* **Featured Regions**: Sunfire Wastes, Frostpeak Mountains, Stormrage Highlands.

### Act III: The Ruined Crown (Levels 32–45, ~25 Hours)
* **Purpose**: High-stakes alliance building and ancient empire exploration.
* **Goals**: Breach Ashen Peaks, explore the Ruined Imperial Capital, and stop the Void Gate ritual.
* **Featured Regions**: Ashen Peaks, Eternian Empire Ruins, Arcane Archipelago.

### Act IV: The Void Siege (Levels 45–55, ~15 Hours)
* **Purpose**: Final campaign climax and endgame boss assault.
* **Goals**: Lead allied forces into the Abyssal Wastes; defeat Malakor the Void Lord.
* **Featured Region**: Abyssal Wastes.
