## 1. Overview & Sector Breakdown

The Citadel of Obsidian Void is an 8-sector final stronghold:

1. **Citadel Outer Breach (`sector_outer_breach`)**: Shattered outer walls, Allied siege camp.
2. **Fortified Gatehouse (`sector_fortified_gatehouse`)**: Iron Dreadnought mini-boss, shortcut gate.
3. **Machine Core & Steam Chambers (`sector_machine_core`)**: Subterranean mechanical foundry.
4. **Void Crystal Corridors (`sector_crystal_corridors`)**: Void Matriarch mini-boss.
5. **Archive of the Sun-Kings (`sector_sun_king_archive`)**: Stolen celestial relics, shortcut gate.
6. **Subterranean Portal Vault (`sector_portal_vault`)**: Archon of Sunless Void mini-boss.
7. **Grand Promenade of Shadows (`sector_grand_promenade`)**: High Commander Vaelis Remnant mini-boss, shortcut gate.
8. **Pre-Final Antechamber of Malakor (`sector_pre_final_antechamber`)**: Sanctuary threshold preceding the Throne Room.
