# ACT II REGIONS — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 31)

## Overview

Act II opens immediately after completing `q_act1_conclusion`. Two new regions become accessible:

| Region | Level Range | Unlocked By |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern Ridgeline | 19–24 | Act I completion |
| Mirkwood Swamps | 21–27 | Liberating the Ridgeline Watchtower |

---

## Eastern Ridgeline (`region_eastern_ridgeline`)

Storm-battered cliffs and mountain passes east of the sealed Citadel of Void Shadows. Malakor's Vanguard forces control key waypoints and have constructed a fortified watchtower that threatens Valen Crown supply lines.

### Key Locations
- **Ridgeline Watchtower** (`poi_ridgeline_watchtower`) — Fort held by Vanguard Captain Drael. Central Act II objective.
- **Shadow Cult Encampment** — Prison holding Seraphine. First major objective.
- **Ridgeline Waystone** — Ancient fast-travel node guarded by the Shadow Emissary.

### Enemies
| Enemy | Level | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow Cult Vanguard | 19 | Standard |
| Ridgeline Harpy | 20 | Aerial |
| Storm Golem | 21 | Elite |
| Vanguard Captain Drael | 22 | **Mini-Boss** |

---

## Mirkwood Swamps (`region_mirkwood_swamps`)

Dense fog-choked wetlands south of the Eastern Ridgeline. Shadow Cult survivors fled here after Vareth's defeat and now regroup under a new shadow commander.

### Key Locations
- **Elda's Herbalist Hut** — Safe zone and merchant.
- **Cult Ritual Grounds** — Shadow Cult reformation site.
- **Sunken Ruin Entrance** — Preview of Act II second dungeon (locked in Prompt 31).

### Enemies
| Enemy | Level | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Mirkwood Lurker | 21 | Ambush |
| Bog Witch | 22 | Spellcaster |
| Plague Shambler | 23 | Tank |

---

## Companion: Seraphine Vael

Seraphine joins the party after `q_act2_ridgeline_rescue`. She brings:
- **Arcane Pulse** — AoE arcane burst
- **Shadow Sight** — Reveals hidden enemies on the minimap
- **Barrier Weave** — Reactive shield for herself or the player

She provides recurring dialogue commentary throughout Act II, reacting to Malakor lore discoveries.
