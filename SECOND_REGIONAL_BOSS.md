# HERO OF ETERNIA — SECOND REGIONAL BOSS: HIGH INQUISITOR VESPER

---

## 1. Boss Encounter Overview

**High Inquisitor Vesper** is the second major regional boss encounter of Act II, guarding the subterranean Sunken Catacombs beneath Eternia Prime.

- **Recommended Level**: Level 27
- **Max Health**: 2,800 HP
- **Armor Mitigation**: 35% damage reduction

---

## 2. Combat Phases & Abilities

1. **Phase 1: Barrier Stage (100% – 70% HP)**
   - Vesper channels *Arcane Barrier Shield*, reducing incoming damage by 70% while casting *Void Flame Nova* (85 damage).
2. **Phase 2: Summoning Stage (70% – 30% HP)**
   - Casts *Shadow Construct Summon*, spawning 3 elite void wraiths while firing targeted shadow bolts.
3. **Phase 3: Desperate Cataclysm (30% – 0% HP)**
   - Channel *Void Cataclysm* (140 damage pulse), creating escalating area denial zones across the boss arena.
