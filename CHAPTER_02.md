# CHAPTER 02 SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 29)

## Chapter 2 Main Storyline & Quests (`Chapter2QuestChain.cs`)

### 1. Whispers in Sylvanwood (`q_sylvanwood_investigation`)
* **Category**: Main
* **Recommended Level**: 6
* **Objective**: Travel to Elderwood Grove and report to Warden Kaelen.
* **Reward**: 400 XP, 100 Gold.

### 2. The Blighted Shrine (`q_corrupted_shrine`)
* **Category**: Main
* **Recommended Level**: 7
* **Objective**: Clear 6 Blighted Forest Boars threatening the leyline shrine.
* **Reward**: 550 XP, 150 Gold.

### 3. Secrets of Aethelgard (`q_ruin_guardian_boss`)
* **Category**: Main (Major Boss)
* **Recommended Level**: 10
* **Objective**: Enter the Ancient Elven Ruins of Aethelgard and defeat the Ancient Ruin Guardian.
* **Reward**: 1000 XP, 400 Gold, Ancient Titan Relic.

### 4. The Fate of Aethelgard Relic (`q_first_choice_decision`)
* **Category**: Main (Major Decision)
* **Recommended Level**: 11
* **Objective**: Choose whether to entrust the relic to Warden Kaelen (Order) or Scholar Elora (Knowledge).
* **Reward**: 800 XP, 300 Gold, Faction Reputation Shift.
