# SECRET SYSTEM SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 25)

## System Overview
`SecretManager.cs` manages secret detection and revelation across world environments.

## Secret Categories
* **HiddenRoom**: Concealed chambers behind false walls.
* **BreakableWall**: Destructible masonry revealed by physical impacts or elemental spells.
* **IllusionaryWall**: Passable magical facades.
* **UndergroundEntrance**: Hidden trapdoors and cellar hatches.
* **InvisibleTrigger**: Proximity-activated secret rewards.
