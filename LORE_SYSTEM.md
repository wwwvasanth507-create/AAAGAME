# LORE SYSTEM SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 25/26)

## System Overview
`LoreManager.cs` provides a historical codex database tracking books, letters, stone tablets, ancient records, audio logs, memory fragments, and timeline entries.

## Categories
* **Book & Letter**: Readable narrative items found in settlements and libraries.
* **StoneTablet**: Ancient ruins stone inscriptions.
* **AncientRecord**: Historical archives and codices.
* **MemoryFragment**: Interactive narrative flashbacks.
* **TimelineEntry**: Historical era chronicles stored in player codex.
