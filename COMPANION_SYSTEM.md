# COMPANION SYSTEM — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 31)

## Overview

Companions are permanent party members who join the Hero during Act II+. They contribute unique abilities, world commentary, and relationship progression.

---

## Companion: Seraphine Vael (`companion_seraphine`)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Class** | Arcane Scout |
| **Join Quest** | `q_act2_ridgeline_rescue` |
| **Start Level** | 18 |
| **Voice Style** | Sharp, observant, dry humor — trusts actions over words |
| **Backstory** | Former Valen Crown intelligence operative who survived the Citadel siege. Holds crucial intel on Malakor's network. |

### Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| `ability_arcane_pulse` | AoE arcane burst damaging nearby enemies |
| `ability_shadow_sight` | Reveals hidden enemies and traps on the minimap for 30s |
| `ability_barrier_weave` | Reactive magical shield protecting Seraphine or the player |

### Dialogue Hooks
- Comments on all Malakor-related lore discoveries
- Unique dialogue at Mirkwood Swamp entry
- Reacts to boss defeats with faction context
