# HERO OF ETERNIA — CORRUPTED FORTRESS: FORTRESS OF OBSIDIAN SHADOWS

---

## 1. Overview & Sector Layout

The Fortress of Obsidian Shadows is a 7-sector handcrafted Act III dungeon:

1. **Outer Battlements & Watchtowers (`sector_fortress_battlements`)**: Alarm gongs and searchlight towers.
2. **Collapsed Courtyard (`sector_fortress_courtyard`)**: Barricade checkpoints and patrol squads.
3. **Void Armory (`sector_fortress_armory`)**: Siege engine depot and explosive barrels.
4. **Prison Catacombs (`sector_fortress_prison`)**: Iron cage cells holding captive allied scouts.
5. **High Command Hall (`sector_fortress_command_hall`)**: Invasion maps and void spellweavers.
6. **Dark Ritual Chamber (`sector_fortress_ritual_chamber`)**: Pulsing void rift crystal altar.
7. **Grand Marshal's War Arena (`sector_fortress_arena`)**: Boss arena for General Vaelis.
