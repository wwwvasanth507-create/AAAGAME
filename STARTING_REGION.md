# STARTING REGION SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 28)

## Oakvale Village & Surrounding Lands

### 1. Key Locations (`StartingRegionContent.cs`)
* **Oakvale Village Square**: Central hub containing the market, elder's hall, and fountain.
* **Training Field**: Combat practice arena managed by Captain Valerius.
* **Thorin's Forge**: Blacksmith workshop for equipment repair and crafting.
* **The Boar & Lantern Inn**: Rest area and innkeeper Elena's tavern.
* **Shrine of Eternia**: Ancient leyline shrine providing blessing buffs and save points.
* **Whispering Cavern Entrance**: Subterranean cave dungeon housing Baron Skarr's bandit gang.
