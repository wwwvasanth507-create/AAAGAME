## 1. High-Level Campaign Structure

Act III shifts the campaign into high-level exploration, dark fantasy themes, advanced mobility, and intense combat across corrupted regions.

- **Level Range**: Level 31 – Level 45
- **Primary Campaign Conflict**: Confronting Arch-Sorcerer Malakor's inner circle in the corrupted core of Eternia.
- **Core Features**: Advanced Traversal (Grapple Hooks, Wall Climbing, Ziplines), Environmental Miasma Hazards, High-Tier Crafting, and Multi-Phase Fortress Raids.
