By the end of this phase, your project should include:

* A world-state system capable of changing NPCs, environments, and gameplay based on narrative progress.
* A cinematic trigger framework ready for future cutscenes.
* A lore management system integrated with the journal and codex.
* Save integration, testing, optimization, and comprehensive documentation.

At this stage, the game has both the **technical infrastructure** and the **narrative framework** needed to begin implementing actual content. **Prompt #27** should logically move into **the Hero of Eternia campaign design**, defining the complete story outline, major regions, chapter breakdown, principal characters, and narrative pacing before writing any individual missions or dialogue.
