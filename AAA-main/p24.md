# Milestone After Prompt #24

After this phase, your project should include:

* A procedural point-of-interest framework.
* Reusable landmark and exploration systems.
* A scalable dungeon architecture ready for future content.
* Rich environmental decoration rules that avoid repetitive layouts.
* Seed-based, reproducible world generation integrated with streaming.
* Exploration tracking and discovery systems connected to saves.
* Android-optimized performance with comprehensive testing and documentation.

At this point, the project shifts from building infrastructure to filling the world with meaningful gameplay. **Prompt #25** should introduce **world activities, puzzles, secrets, collectibles, environmental interactions, and dynamic exploration events**, making exploration consistently rewarding without depending on the main story.
