## 1. Scope & Citadel Puzzle Systems

The Citadel of Obsidian Void integrates advanced multi-sector puzzle networks:

- **Void Energy Routing (`puzzle_void_energy_routing`)**: Redirect dark energy beams in Sector 3 (Machine Core) to overload shield barriers.
- **Rune Gate Keys (`puzzle_citadel_key_array`)**: Defeat the 4 Citadel Mini-Bosses to obtain the 4 Sun-King Rune Keys required to unseal Malakor's Antechamber.
