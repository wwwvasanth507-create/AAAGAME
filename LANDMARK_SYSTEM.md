# LANDMARK SYSTEM SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 24)

## System Overview
`LandmarkDatabase.cs` and `LandmarkDefinition.cs` manage world landmark points, navigation beacons, scenic viewpoints, exploration rewards, and visual uniqueness scoring.

## Landmark Categories
* **Major**: Legendary world spires, titan statues, and massive ancient structures.
* **Minor**: Regional watchtowers, stone circles, and prominent waterfalls.
* **NavigationMarker**: Crossroads signs and mountain pass guide stones.
* **ScenicViewpoint**: High-elevation vistas triggering exploration XP rewards.
