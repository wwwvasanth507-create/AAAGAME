# CHAPTER 03 SPECIFICATION — HERO OF ETERNIA (PROMPT 30)

## Chapter 3: Into the Void Citadel

### Overview
Chapter 3 is the Act I climax. Players prepare in Elderwood Grove, then enter the **Citadel of Void Shadows** — a multi-floor handcrafted dungeon built atop the Ancient Elven Ruins of Aethelgard. The chapter concludes with the defeat of **Commander Vareth the Void Knight** and a triumphant return to Oakvale.

---

## Quest Chain (`Chapter3QuestChain.cs`)

### 1. Before the Darkness Falls (`q_chapter3_preparation`)
* **Level**: 13 | **Reward**: 600 XP, 200 Gold
* Speak with Warden Kaelen, stock supplies, and activate the Citadel travel waypoint.

### 2. Into the Void Citadel (`q_citadel_entry`)
* **Level**: 14 | **Reward**: 900 XP, 250 Gold
* Traverse the Shadow Corridor, solve the Rune Pressure Chamber, survive the Void Spike Gauntlet, and reach Floor 2.

### 3. The Shadow Knight's Trial (`q_shadow_knight_miniboss`)
* **Level**: 15 | **Reward**: 1,200 XP, 350 Gold
* Defeat the Shadow Knight guardian in the Crucible arena.

### 4. Commander Vareth — Void Knight (`q_void_knight_boss`)
* **Level**: 17 | **Reward**: 2,500 XP, 700 Gold, Void-Cleaved Broadsword
* Three-phase boss encounter at the Throne of the Void Gate.

### 5. Act I — The Hero's Oath (`q_act1_conclusion`)
* **Level**: 18 | **Reward**: 3,000 XP, 1,000 Gold, 500 Reputation
* Return to Elder Alden. Act I complete.
