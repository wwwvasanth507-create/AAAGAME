# HERO OF ETERNIA — SHADOW FRONTIER REGION GUIDE

---

## 1. Overview & Sub-Zone Breakdown

The Shadow Frontier is an expansive Act III high-level region comprising 7 hazardous sub-zones:

1. **Corrupted Whispering Woods (`zone_corrupted_woods`)**: Toxic spore miasma and shadow stalkers.
2. **Dread Ravine (`zone_dread_ravine`)**: Deep cliffside chasm requiring grapple hook navigation.
3. **Ruined Fort Ironwood (`zone_ruined_fort_ironwood`)**: Desolated bastion overrun by corrupted iron knights.
4. **Ashen Battlefield (`zone_ashen_battlefield`)**: Ancient plains guarded by obelisk wardens.
5. **Gloomstone Caverns (`zone_gloomstone_caverns`)**: Subterranean crystal caves with shadow crawlers.
6. **Blighted Marshlands (`zone_blighted_marshlands`)**: Poisonous swamplands requiring rope bridges.
7. **Obsidian Crag Sanctuary (`zone_obsidian_crag_sanctuary`)**: Mountain sanctuary housing ancient Eternian secrets.
